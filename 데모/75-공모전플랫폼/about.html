About GYCA

A fair stage,
judged in the open

GYCA is an international creative awards platform for makers aged 8 to 22.

How judging works

Three stages,
named jurors

Who judges, when, and by what measure — all public.

STAGE 1

Field panel

Regional experts in each field review anonymized entries and shortlist by the published rubric.

STAGE 2

Cross-review

A second panel scores the shortlist. Per-juror score deviations are normalized so no one's harsh or generous scale decides a grade.

FINAL

Grade & reveal

Grades are assigned, ties resolved by rule, and names revealed only after scoring closes.

Scoring, published

Every entry, five criteria

The same rubric applies to every field.

Trust

Not a pay-to-play

Fee waiver

If cost is a barrier, request a waiver — no proof required. A young creator's chance shouldn't depend on a fee.

Copyright stays yours

We never claim your work. Public display follows the permissions you choose at submission.

Safe for minors

Guardian consent, name masking, and blind judging protect younger creators by default.
