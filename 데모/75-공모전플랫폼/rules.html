Participation Rules

How entries, judging, grades and rights work at GYCA.

1. Eligibility

Entrants must be aged 8 to 22 at the submission deadline. Individuals and teams may enter. Team members may be of different ages or countries; the youngest member sets the consent requirements for the team.

2. Entries and originality

Each entry must be the entrant's own original work. Entries created substantially by another person may be disqualified. One work may be entered in one field per cycle; each entry is a separate submission with its own fee.

3. Blind judging and conflicts

Entries are anonymized before judging — jurors see an entry number and a file with author metadata removed. Jurors must recuse themselves from any entry linked to them. Per-juror score deviations are normalized so no single scale decides a grade.

4. Grades, ties and results

Grades are Grand Prize, Gold, Silver, Bronze and Finalist. Ties within a grade quota are resolved by the published tie-break rule before results are announced. Results are revealed at the scheduled time; premature disclosure is prevented by a time lock.

5. Fees, waivers and refunds

The entry fee is stated on each competition page. A fee waiver may be requested without proof. Refunds are available before judging begins. A minor may not pay alone — a guardian completes or approves payment.

6. Rights and public display

Copyright in every entry stays with the entrant. GYCA does not acquire ownership of any entry. Winning works may be shown in the gallery, exhibitions, publications and press — but only for the specific uses the entrant allows at submission.

7. Overseas final

Grade winners may be invited to an overseas final. Total cost, including any support by grade, is disclosed in full before the entrant decides. Not attending the final does not revoke a grade.
