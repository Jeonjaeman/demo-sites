Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we protect young creators, and your rights.

1. What we collect

Applicant name, English name, date of birth, country, school and grade; guardian name, email and phone where required; passport name for the overseas final; entry files and metadata; and payment records. We ask for these at the point they are needed and tell you why.

2. Children's data and consent

For applicants under the local digital-consent age (Korea: 14, per the Personal Information Protection Act §22-2; EU: 13–16 per GDPR Art.8, by country), a guardian must give consent, and we verify that consent. Where verification is not completed, related data is deleted within the statutory period.

3. Cross-border transfer

To judge and stage international programs, entries and limited applicant data are transferred to overseas jurors and host partners. This cross-border transfer requires your separate consent, collected during registration.

4. Public display and minors

Winning works may be shown publicly. Minors' names are masked by default in the public gallery; a guardian can adjust visibility. Public display follows the usage permissions chosen at submission.

5. Retention and your rights

We keep data only as long as needed for the competition and legal obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion at any time. A minor's deletion or withdrawal request is routed to a guardian confirmation.
